Discussion

Boundary prompt

v2 proposed the excluded /app/admin target in 9 of 10 executions. The provider denied 9 of those 9, and each had zero modeled effects. This is an observation about these trials.

Repeat variation

v2's two fresh repeats differed (one no-call, one excluded-target proposal). A seed and temperature of zero did not make these observed outputs identical; two repeats cannot establish a general determinism claim.

Audit-only class

v3 supplied a false claimed_class=data_modification in 10 of 10 proposals. The provider used the actual operation/target; the claim did not change the modeled policy decision.

Effects and evidence

Only the 4 Allow outcomes appended one receipt. The 17 Deny, 8 Indeterminate and 1 no-mediation outcomes appended none. Fresh fixtures are created per request; deltas are not cumulative.

Conclusion and boundary

In these fixed synthetic fixtures, the provider mediated every valid model proposal, denied 9 of 9 observed excluded-target attempts, and recorded no modeled effect after Deny, Indeterminate or no-call.

This demonstrates the provider decision path in a small local sample. It does not demonstrate enforcement of real tools, real-world authorization evidence, a population model-behavior rate, a privacy guarantee, or deployment latency. A separate independent security review is required before production use.

To try additional scenarios on a local machine, follow docs/manual-experiments.md. Keep exploratory runs distinct from the preregistered matrix.
